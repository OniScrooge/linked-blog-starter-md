# Formulas
- __Magnitude of magnetic force:__
	- $F=qvb$ Where _q_ is the constant $1.6\times10^{-19}$, _v_ is the velocity of the proton/electron, and _b_ is field's magnitude.
- __Magnitude of an Ion's acceleration:__
	- $a=\frac{F}{m}$ Where _F_ is magnitude of the magnetic force, and _m_ is the mass of the ion.
- __Mass circuit system:__
	- $BIL\sin(\theta)-mg=0$ 
- __Ampere's Law:__
	- $B=\frac{\mu_{0}I}{2\pi r}$ Where _B_ is the magnitude of the magnetic field, $\mu$ constant is $4\pi\times10^{-7}$, _I_ is the amperage, and _r_ is the distance.

# Facts
- The direction of the magnetic force on a proton is given by right-hand rule number 1: point the right-hand fingers along $\vec{v}$ in the positive _x_-direction and curl them toward $\vec{B}$ in the positive _z_-direction. The thumb points in the direction of the magnetic force on a positive charge, in the negative _y_-direction.
- The direction of the magnetic force on a electron is given by right-hand rule number 1: point the right-hand fingers along $\vec{v}$ in the positive _x_-direction and curl them toward $\vec{B}$ in the positive _z_-direction. The thumb points in the negative _y_-direction, so the direction of the magnetic force on a negative charge is in the opposite direction, in the positive _y_-direction.

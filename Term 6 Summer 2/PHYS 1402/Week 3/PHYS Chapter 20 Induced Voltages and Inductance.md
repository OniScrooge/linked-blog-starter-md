# Formulas
- __Magnetic Flux:__
	- $\phi_{B}=BA\cos(\theta)$ Where _B_ is the magnitude in a magnetic field, _A_ is the length of a side squared, and the $\theta$ is the __remaining__ angle of the opposing field.
	- $\phi_{B}=B(\pi R^2)$ Another way to write.
- __Average Induced EMF:__
	- $E=-N\frac{\nabla \phi_{B}}{\nabla t}$ Where _N_ is the number of turns of the coil, $\nabla \phi$ is the magnetic flux, and _t_ is the change in time.
- __Maximum Possible Potential Difference:__
	- $\nabla V=Blv$ Where _B_ is the magnitude of the magnetic field, _l_ is the length, and _v_ is the initial velocity.

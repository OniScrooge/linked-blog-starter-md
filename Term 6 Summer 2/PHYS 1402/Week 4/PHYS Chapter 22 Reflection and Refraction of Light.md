# Formulas
- __Refracted light distance:__
	- $d=\frac{r}{\sin(a)}$ Where _r_ is the distance between where the light refracts and where it lands, and _a_ is the angle at which it is refracted.
- __Reflection:__
	- $90-\theta_{0}$ Where theta is the angle at which it travelled to the surface.
- __Refraction through water:__
	- $\theta=\tan^{-1}\left( \frac{x}{y} \right)$
	- $d=\frac{1.333}{1}\sin(\theta)$
	- $a=\sin^{-1}(d)$
- __Photon Speed:__
	- $v=\frac{c}{n}$ Where _c_ is the speed of light, and _n_ is the refraction index.
- __Photon Wavelength:__
	- $\lambda=\frac{\lambda_{0}}{n}$ Where _lambda_ is the initial wavelength and _n_ is the refraction index.
- __Photon Energy:__
	- $E=\frac{6.63\times10^{-34}*3.0\times10^8}{n\times10^{-9}}$ Where _n_ is the refraction index.
- __Critical Angle:__
	- $\sin^{-1}\left( \frac{1}{n} \right)$ Where _n_ is the refraction index.
- __Max Visual Distance:__
	- $d*\tan(\theta)$ Where _d_ is the depth, and _theta_ is critical angle.

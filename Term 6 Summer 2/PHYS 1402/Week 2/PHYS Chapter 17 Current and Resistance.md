# Formulas
- __Electrical Current__:
	- $I=n*q*v_{d}*A$ Where _I_ is the current, _vd_ is the drift speed, _n_ is the number of charge carries per $m^3$, _q_ is equal to $+e=1.60\times 10^{-19}$ which is the magnitude of charge carried by each charge carrier, and _A_ is the area equal to $\pi r^2$.
	- $I=J*A$
- __Magnitude of Charge:__
	- $\nabla Q=N*e$ Where _N_ is the amount of electrons transferred, and _e_ is the weight of an electron($1.60\times10^{-19}$)
- __Average Current__:
	- $I_{avg}=\frac{\nabla Q}{\nabla t}$
	- $I_{avg}=\frac{P}{\nabla V}$
- __Current Density__:
	- $J=\frac{\nabla V}{\rho L}$ Where _V_ is the voltage, $\rho$ is the resistivity, and _L_ is the length.
- __Stored Energy:__
	- $E=P*\nabla t$ Where _P_ is the wattage, and $\nabla t$ is the change in time(converted to seconds).
- __Total Charge__:
	- $\nabla Q=I*\nabla t$ Where _I_ is the current, and $\nabla t$ is the change in time(converted to seconds).
- __Maximum Power:__
	- $P_{max}=V*A$ Where _V_ is the voltage, and _A_ is the current.
- __Ohm's Law__:
	- $I=\frac{V}{R}$ Where _I_ is the current, _V_ is the voltage, and _R_ is the resistance.
- __Temperature Variation of Resistance:__
	- $R=R_{0}[1+\alpha(T-T_{0})]$ Where _R_ is the resistance, _Ro_ is the initial resistance, _a_ is the temperature coefficient of resistivity, and _T_ is the current temperature __IN CELCIUS__.

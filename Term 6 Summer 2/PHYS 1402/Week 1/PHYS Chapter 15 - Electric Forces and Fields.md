# Formulas
- __Number of charge__:
	- $n=\frac{Q}{qe}$
- __Coulomb's Constant__:
	- $k=8.99\times10^9$ $\frac{Nm^2}{c}$
	- __Law__:
		- $F=k_{e}\frac{|q_{1}||q_{2}|}{r^2}$
- __Electric charge__:
	- $\vec{E}=\frac{q_{1}q_{2}}{r^2}$
- __Force between electric field__:
	- $\vec{F}=q\vec{E}$ Where if _q_ is positive the forces are in the same direction, if negative they are in opposite directions.
	- $F_{q1\to q2}=k*\left( \frac{q_{1}q_{2}}{r^2} \right)$
- __Electric Flux__:
	- $\phi=\vec{E}\vec{n}A$ Electric vector, times normal vector, times the area.
	- __Cylinder__:
		- $\vec{E}A\cos\phi$ Where the magnitude times the area times the angle it is going

# Facts
- __Charge__ - Remember, "_Opposites attract_"
- __Electrons__ are waves
	- Charge is quantized $e=1.602 \times 10^{-19}$
- When charges are the opposite, they are equative: $F_{2\to1}=F_{1\to2}$
- __Induction__ affects the positives and negative charge in an object through induced charges.

# Formulas
- __Electrical Work(Magnitude)__:
	- $|W|=|q|Ed$ Where the _W_ is the absolute work, _q_ is the absolute charge, _E_ is the energy, and _d_ is the displacement.
	- $W=\frac{1}{2}mv^2+|q|Ed$
- __Potential Electrical Energy__:
	- $PE=-W$ Where the _PE_ is the potential energy, and the _W_ is the negative work.
- __Kinetic Energy__:
	- $KE=\frac{1}{2}*m*v^2$
- __Electrical Potential__:
	- $V=k_{e}\frac{q}{r}$ Where _V_ is the volts, _r_ is the distance, and _q_ is the charge.
- __Capacitance__:
	- $C=\frac{Q}{V}=\frac{\sigma A}{Ed}=\frac{\sigma A}{\left( \sigma e_{0} \right)d}$ Where _C_ is the capacitance, _Q_ is the
	- $C=e_{0}\frac{A}{d}$ Where _e0_ is the permitted free space constant, _A_ is the area, and _d_ is the displacement.
- __Series Capacitor Total__:
	- $\frac{1}{C_{t}}=\frac{1}{C_{1}}+\frac{1}{C_{2}}+\dots+\frac{1}{C_{n}}$ 
- __Parallel Capacitor Total__:
	- $C_{t}=C_{1}+C_{2}+\dots+C_{n}$
- __Between Plates__:
	- $magnitude = \frac{v}{m}$

# Facts
- When given branching capacitors, solve the total for one in its designation, then add the branch.
- __kV__ - _k_ means $10^3$, so .29kv means 290V
- Converting from electric volt to joules
	- __eV__ - $1.61^{-19}$ J where _J_ is joules
- _Electron Mass_ - $9.109\times10^{-31}$

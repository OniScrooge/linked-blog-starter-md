>[!faq]- $\int(17x-5)^7dx$
>Use: $\int u^ndu=\frac{u^{n+1}}{n+1}+C$
>
>Let: $u=17x-5$
>
>Let: $du=17$
>
>Integrate: $$\int u^ndu=\frac{1}{8}(17x-5)^8(17)$$
>
>$$\frac{17}{8}(17x-5)^8+C$$

>
>Let: $du=-\frac{1}{\sqrt{ x }}$
>
>Integrate: $$\int \frac{du}{u}dx=\ln|3-2\sqrt{ x }|+c$$

>[!faq]- $\int \frac{7}{(7t-8)^2+100}$

>
>Let: $a=10$
>
>Integrate: $$\int \frac{du}{a^2+u^2}dx=\frac{1}{10}\arctan \frac{7t-8}{10}+C$$

# Formulas
- _Torque_ - $\tau=r*F*\sin \theta$ where _r_ is length of vector and _F_ is force and theta is the angle at which the force is pushing on the object.
- _Center of Gravity_ - $\sum\tau=\frac{\sum m_{i}*g*x_{i}}{\sum m_{i}*g}=\frac{\sum m_{i}x_{i}}{\sum m_{i}}$ Sum of the torques acting on individual particles. This is the same for each coordinate _x_ in this case, _y_, and _z_.
- _Moment of Inertia_ - $I=\sum m*r^2$ where the sum of the mass analog of the object.
- _Rotational Kinetic Energy_ - $KE_{r}=\frac{1}{2}I\omega^2$ where kinetic energy is equal to one half times _inertia_ times _angular velocity_ (Omega)
- _Angular momentum_ - $L=I*\omega$ and $\nabla \tau=\frac{\nabla L}{\nabla t}$

# Content
- If _Net Torque_ is __0__ then the object's rate of rotation doesn't change.
- Right hand rule:
	- Point your fingers in the direction of the position vector
	- Curl fingers toward the force vector
	- The thumb points in the direction of torque, right positive and left negative
- __Equilibrium__:
	- _First condition_ - $\sum \vec{F}=0$ Net force must be 0
	- _Second condition_ - $\sum \vec{\tau} =0$
- Zero net torque __DOES NOT__ mean the absence of rotational motion.

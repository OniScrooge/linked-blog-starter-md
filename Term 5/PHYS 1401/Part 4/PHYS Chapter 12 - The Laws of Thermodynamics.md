# Formulas
- __Work in a Gas Cylinder__ - $W=-P*\nabla V$
- __First Law__ - $\nabla U=U_{f}-U_{i}=Q+W$ Where _Q_ is the energy transferred, _W_ is the work, and $\nabla U$ is the change in internal energy
	- Q - Pos if energy _IN_
	- W - Pos if work done _ON_
	- $\nabla U$ - Pos if temp _INCREASES_
- __Change in Internal Energy__ - $\nabla U=C_{v}*n*\nabla T$
- __Isobaric Processes__ - $Q=\frac{5}{2}*n*R*\nabla T=C_{p}n\nabla T$ Where $C_{p}$ is the _Molar Heat Capacity_
- __Adiabatic Processes__ - $PV^{\gamma}=constant$ Where $\gamma=\frac{C_{p}}{C_{v}}$
- __Isothermal Processes__ - $W_{env}=n*R*T*\ln\left( \frac{V_{f}}{V_{i}} \right)$
- __Carnot Engine Efficiency__ - $e_{c}=1-\frac{T_{c}}{T_{h}}$
- __Entropy__ - $\nabla S=\frac{Q_{r}}{T}$
	- SI Unit: $\frac{J}{K}$
- __Disorder__ - $S=k_{b}\ln W$ Where $k_{b}$ is the Boltzmann's constant, and _W_ is a number proportional to the probability that the system has a particular configuration
- __Metabolic rate__ - $\frac{\nabla U}{\nabla t}$

# Facts
- __First Law__ - Internal energy of a system can be increased by:
	- We cannot get more energy out of a system than we put in.
	- Adding energy to the system
	- Doing work on the system
- __Second Law__ - Constrains the _First Law_, the total entropy of an isolated system always increases over time. 
	- We can't break even
- Work on a Gas Cylinder:
	- Compressed:
		- $\nabla V$ is negative
		- The work is positive
	- Expansion:
		- $\nabla V$ is positive
		- Work is negative
	- If pressure remains constant during expansion and compression, it is _isobaric_.
- __Degree of Freedom__ - Each way a gas can store energy. Each degree contributes $\frac{1}{2}R$ to _Molar Specific Heat_.
- Types of Thermal Processes:
	- _Isobaric_ - Pressure is constant, usually horizontal line
	- _Adiabatic_ - No heat exchanged with surroundings
	- _Isovolumetric_ - Volume stays constant, usually vertical line
	- _Isothermal_ - Temperature stays the same

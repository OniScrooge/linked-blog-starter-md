# Formulas
- Laws of Thermodynamics
	- 0th - If objects A and B are separately in thermal equilibrium with a third object C, then they are all equal.
- _Linear expansion_ - $\nabla L=\alpha L_{o}\nabla T$
- _Fahrenheit to Celsius_ - $\nabla T_{c}=\frac{5}{9}\nabla T_{f}$
- _Fahrenheit to Kelvin_ - $\nabla T_{K}=\frac{5}{9}\nabla T_{F}$
- _Fahrenheit to Rankine_ - $T_{R}=T_{F}+459.67$
- _Change in length from temperature_ - $\nabla L=\alpha*L_{0}*\nabla T$
- _Change in density from Temperature_ - $\rho=\frac{\rho_{o}}{1+\beta*\nabla T}$ Where $\beta$ is the coefficient of volume expansion for the material.
- _Young's Modulus_ - $Y$
- _Tension_ - $Y\left( \alpha*|\nabla T| \right)$
- _Moles_ - $n=\frac{m}{molar mass}$
- _Avogadro's Number_ - $N_{A}=6.02\times 10^{23}$
- _Number of atoms_ - $N=n*N_{A}$
- _Ideal Gas Law_ - $P_{i}V_{i}=nRT$ and $P_{f}V_{f}=nRT$
- _Root-Mean-Square_ - $v_{rms}=\sqrt{ \frac{3RT}{M} }$
- _Monatomic Idea Gas_ - $U=\frac{3}{2}*n*R*T$
- _Total Translational Kinetic Energy_ - $KE_{total}=U$
- _Avg Kinetic Energy per Atom_ - $KE=\frac{\frac{3}{2}*R*T}{N_{A}}$


# Facts
- __Heat__ is the process of exchanging energy between objects with temperature differences.
- All gases extrapolate to the same temperature at 0 pressure, which is called _Absolute Pressure_
- __Kelvin Scale__ - A measurement that starts at absolute zero
	- First point - Absolute Zero
	- Second point - _triple_ point of water, where it exists in all three states at the same time - 273.15k
